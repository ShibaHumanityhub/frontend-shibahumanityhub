Privacy

Last updated: 22 September 2026. This is a plain-language working draft for the public site. It is not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Blueprint email. If you ask for The Mercy Blueprint, we receive the address you submit through Formspree (form endpoint on the homepage).
  • Messages. If you write us via the contact form or X, we see what you send.
  • Wallet (optional). If you connect a wallet on this site, the connection is designed as read-only so you can see balances and circles. We do not ask you to sign transfers from this marketing site.
  • Basic analytics / hosting. Our host (Netlify) and standard web logs may see IP, browser, and pages visited.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your email list.
  • We do not pretend charity delivery is live before rails and approvals are real.
  • We do not ask for seed phrases or private keys. Ever.

Why we ask

Email is so we can send the Blueprint and, later, honest updates about status, live moments, and proof when rails are live. You can ask to be removed.

Third parties

Formspree processes Blueprint submissions. X / Twitter is a separate platform if you follow or message us there. Blockchain explorers (for example shibariumscan.io) are public when you view contracts.

Your choices

  • Use the site without submitting email.
  • Open the Blueprint page without the form.
  • Request deletion of an email you submitted: use Contact and say so clearly.

Children

This site is for adults and guardians. We do not knowingly collect emails from children.

Changes

When charity registration, banking, or delivery rails go live, we will update this page. Truth first.
