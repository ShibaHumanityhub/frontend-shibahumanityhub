What this policy covers

This page explains how we handle personal information on shibahumanityhub.com and related pages we control. It is not a substitute for advice from your own counsel. Nothing here is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice about tokens or holding.

This policy covers the site as it works today, including the live Mercy Blueprint email capture. It is not deferred until charity approval.

Information we collect

Depending on what you do on the site, we may collect:

  • Email address when you submit the Mercy Blueprint form
  • Messages and contact details you send us once contact channels exist
  • Basic technical data (such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited) from our hosting provider or analytics tools if enabled
  • Publicly visible wallet addresses if you choose a read-only wallet connect

We do not ask you for private keys. Never paste a seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private key into our site or any form we provide.

We do not knowingly invent or treat prototype shelter cards, demo names, or Beautiful Souls clocks as live personal case files about real beneficiaries.
