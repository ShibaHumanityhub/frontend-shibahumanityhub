## 1. Sitewide status chips

Use one chip per claim surface (hero promise, program card, CTA, ledger row, rotator). Chips are labels of *operating reality*, not marketing mood.

| Chip | Exact label | 1-line tooltip / helper |
|------|-------------|-------------------------|
| **Live** | `Live` | Real and usable today: on-chain contract, public page, or verified process you can complete now. |
| **In progress** | `In progress` | Work started; not finished. Charity rails, receipts, partner agreements, legal framing. |
| **Prototype** | `Prototype` | Demo / vision UI for learning. Simulated data; not a live placement, sponsor feed, or delivery. |
| **Planned** | `Planned` | Intended after rails, funding, and partners. Do not present as available. |

### Usage rules

**Use `Live` only for:**  
- Contract addresses and Shibarium explorer links that resolve.  
- Read-only wallet connect for balances (if that path works without promising impact).  
- Public pages that actually load (homepage, whitepaper, journey pages that exist).  
- Blueprint *email capture form* as a live capture (not as “delivery of funded programs”).

**Use `In progress` for:**  
- Charity / nonprofit rails, treasury custody, receipts, full legal framing.  
- Partner shelter agreements, ground delivery pipelines.  
- “When rails are live” funding → convert → deliver path.

**Use `Prototype` for:**  
- Beautiful Souls clocks and shelter bio cards until partner feeds are real.  
- Ripples simulator, Mercy Wheel demo spins / sim chat, Living Ledger preview rows.  
- Healing Hearts pipeline lanes (page already says demos).  
- Any “Save a Life” or sponsorship UI that does not complete a real funded placement.

**Use `Planned` for:**  
- Pod sponsorship with attestations, soulbound passports, Zama / FHE privacy layer.  
- Trip raffles, official live-stream Mercy Wheel seasons.  
- Full 30-program constellation delivery, Global Disaster K9 standby units as operated teams.

**What never sits under `Live`:**  
- Ground delivery (food, vet, guardians, orphans, therapy placements).  
- Charity status or “approved rails.”  
- Sponsorship that moves money to a beneficiary today.  
- “Helping now,” “saves lives” as a present-tense operational claim.  
- Partner names, euthanasia clocks, or “at risk” urgency as verified live cases (unless a signed partner feed exists — it does not yet).  
- Metrics, lives saved, or compounded “Est. lives / 12 mo” as real outcomes.

## 3. Sponsor / Pay It Forward CTA rules

### Do
- Label sponsorship surfaces `Planned` or `In progress` until rails + partner + receipt path exist.  
- Use interest CTAs: “Express interest”, “Join the waitlist”, “Read the design”, “Follow when rails go live.”  
- Keep Pay It Forward’s own honesty: “Motion vision · not a live sponsor feed” / “Receipts later.”  
- Separate **token hold** (belonging) from **care spend** (steady currency when rails are live).  
- Protect dignity: no fake euthanasia countdowns, no invented shelter partners, no “at risk” urgency theater on prototype cards.  
- If a user clicks Sponsor, land on a short truth screen: what is live, what is not, how to stay in touch.

### Don’t
- Don’t say “Sponsor with love” in a way that implies payment completes care today.  
- Don’t use “Save a Life”, “Save Soul”, or “real impact” on demo bios.  
- Don’t mix read-only wallet connect with a sponsorship success state.  
- Don’t show partner shelter names as confirmed when outreach is still “in progress.”  
- Don’t promise monthly dollar care (`$350 Mo`) as an offered product — keep “design target” language.  
- Don’t imply tax-deductible donation status before charity rails and counsel say so.

### Safe CTA microcopy (sponsorship NOT live)

- “Follow this program — sponsorship opens when rails and partners are live.”  
- “Express interest in Pay It Forward (not a live sponsor feed yet).”  
- “Read how one bond is designed to chain — receipts come later.”  
- “Hold with the mission if you choose. Delivery waits for rails. NFA.”  
- “Get the Mercy Blueprint — vision and program design, not a live placement.”  

### Forbidden implications
- That clicking Sponsor funds a named dog/child right now.  
- That holding `$NIBBLES` / `$hopeseed` already pays vet bills or rent.  
- That demo ledger rows or sim chat are real transfers or real saves.  
- That charity status is complete.  
- That urgency clocks on Beautiful Souls are live partner data.
