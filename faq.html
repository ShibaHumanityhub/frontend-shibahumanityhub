FAQ

Straight answers to the things people ask us most.

Is Shiba Humanity Hub live?

Parts are live. The mercy delivery system is not. Live today: the public site, our incorporated entity story, $NIBBLES and $hopeseed contracts on Shibarium, Blueprint email capture, and vision or prototype pages that load. In progress: charity rails, receipts, partner agreements. Not live: ground delivery, verified sponsorships, or “helping now” as an operating claim. Demo-only surfaces get a Prototype chip.

Where does money go?

Design intent: a small transaction tax is meant to route toward an Impact Wallet when tax and charity rails are live. Today: contracts are live; impact routing and receipt-backed delivery are not a finished rail yet. We will not say funds already feed ground programs until that path is real and auditable.

What are the tokens? (NFA)

Two tokens. One heart. $NIBBLES is designed for animal-side programs when funded. $hopeseed is designed for human-side programs when funded. Circles of Mercy are belonging thresholds, not a paycheck or guaranteed return. Not financial advice.

Can I sponsor a dog or child today?

Not as a live paid placement. Sponsorship is planned / in progress. Pay It Forward is motion vision, not a live sponsor feed. Treat “Sponsor a Program” as interest until marked Live with rails and receipts.

Can I donate today?

No counsel-approved, receipt-ready public donation checkout is claimed as live today. Holding tokens is not a charitable donation. When a path opens, it will be labeled Live.

What is the Mercy Blueprint email form?

Optional capture so we can send Blueprint materials. That capture is live. Email for Blueprint materials only. Not a live donation or sponsorship.

Why do some pages say Prototype?

Demo or vision UI. Simulated data. Not a live placement, sponsor feed, or delivery.

What do the status chips mean?

Here is what each label on the site means:

  • Live: Real and usable today: on-chain contract, public page, or verified process you can complete now.
  • In progress: Work started; not finished. Charity rails, receipts, partner agreements, legal framing.
  • Prototype: Demo / vision UI for learning. Simulated data; not a live placement, sponsor feed, or delivery.
  • Planned: Intended after rails, funding, and partners. Do not present as available.
