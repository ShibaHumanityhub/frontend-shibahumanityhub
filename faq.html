FAQ

Short answers for strangers, skeptics, and people who love animals and kids more than jargon.

What is Shiba Humanity Hub?

A mercy mission with two doors: $NIBBLES for dogs and the people they heal, and $hopeseed for children and families. Holding is meant to grow capacity. When rails are live, activity is designed to fund real care with receipts.

What is live right now?

Live: the story site, program pages, and on-chain contracts you can open on Shibarium explorers. In progress: charity rails, banking, and full receipting. Design / preview: simulators, demo ripples, and animated program visions. Delivery claims wait for rails.

How can I help today without buying tokens?

Read The Mercy Blueprint. Meet faces on Beautiful Souls. Follow live moments on X. Partners can explore Barn pods and sponsorship paths. Holding stays optional until you are ready.

What are Circles of Mercy?

Designed belonging lanes at 25,000, 100,000, and 250,000 tokens held. Same thresholds across programs. They are about standing with the mission when funded, not a paycheck and not a guarantee.

What will the first live transfer look like?

When rails are real: a verifiable on-chain move, a clear program destination, and a receipt path you can check. Until then the ledger stays quiet on purpose. No ghost mercy.

What is shibahumanityhub.shib?

A Web3-style name pointing at this home. You can use the normal website today. The .shib name is an identity on that stack, not a second mystery product.

Where do I start if I only have five minutes?

Home → pick Help dogs or Help kids → open one seed story (Healing Hearts, Global K9, Pay It Forward, or Orphan Christmas) → Blueprint or Beautiful Souls.
