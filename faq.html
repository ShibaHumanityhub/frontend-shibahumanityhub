1. Is Shiba Humanity Hub live?

Parts are live; the mercy delivery system is not. Live today: incorporated entity story, public site, $NIBBLES and $hopeseed contracts on Shibarium, Blueprint email capture, and several vision / prototype journey pages. In progress: charity rails, receipts, partner agreements. Not live: ground delivery, verified sponsorship placements, and “helping now” as an operational claim. When a feature is demo-only, we aim to mark it Prototype.

2. Where does money go?

Design intent (whitepaper / Blueprint): a small transaction tax is meant to route to an Impact Wallet to fund programs when tax and charity rails are live, with a portion designed as ecosystem burn. Today: contracts are live; impact routing, conversion to steady care currency, and receipt-backed delivery are not activated as a finished charity rail. We will not claim funds already feed programs on the ground until that path is real and auditable.

3. What are the tokens? (NFA)

Two tokens, one mission framing: $NIBBLES (animal-side programs when funded) and $hopeseed (human-side programs when funded). Circles of Mercy describe belonging thresholds, not a paycheck or guaranteed return. Not financial advice. Do your own research. We do not invent APY, price targets, or “number go up” promises.

4. Can I sponsor a dog or child today?

No. Sponsorship is planned / in progress, not a live paid placement funnel. Journey pages such as Pay It Forward are motion vision — not a live sponsor feed. “Sponsor” CTAs should be read as interest / waitlist energy until marked Live with rails and receipts. Start at Programs.

5. Can I donate today?

There is no counsel-approved, receipt-ready public donation checkout claimed as live on the site today. Holding tokens is not the same as a charitable donation. If and when a donation path opens, it will be labeled clearly (Live), with entity details and receipt practice — not buried in a simulator.

6. What is the Mercy Blueprint email form?

Optional email capture via a form provider so we can deliver Blueprint materials. Live as a mailing/download capture only. Not a live sponsorship. Details: Privacy.

7. Why do some pages say Prototype?

Beautiful Souls clocks, Ripples, Mercy Wheel demos, Living Ledger previews, and some pipelines are demos for learning. Simulated data is not a live placement, sponsor feed, or delivery.
